About AZ Data

A small consultancy,
built to stay small.

AZ Data was started to fix a specific pattern: fast-growing fintechs outrunning their data infrastructure, then discovering it during an audit or a board meeting instead of on their own terms. We take narrow, verifiable engagements — warehouse migrations, pipeline rebuilds, governance — and hand over something your own team can run without us.

Founder

Who's behind AZ Data

Shahin Yusifli

Founder & Principal Data Engineer

Shahin founded AZ Data after years of building and hardening data platforms for fintech and other regulated businesses — the kind of environment where a broken pipeline isn't just an inconvenience, it's a compliance question.

AZ Data grew out of a simple observation: most warehouse problems aren't technical, they're organizational — nobody owns the schema, nobody trusts the numbers, and every migration turns into a fire drill. The consultancy exists to fix that directly, then get out of the way.
