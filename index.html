Why it's different

A context layer, not another catalog.

Catalogs answer "what tables exist" for humans, on a schedule measured in hours. Agents need to know "can I act on this table right now." That takes freshness in minutes, a machine-readable format, and business meaning — not just column names.

  • Polls INFORMATION_SCHEMA, which has no replication lag — not ACCOUNT_USAGE
  • Every read carries a last-synced timestamp, so freshness is verifiable
  • Business annotations travel with the schema into Git and MCP
